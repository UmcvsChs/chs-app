# CHS-Approved Agent Badge — Eligibility, Conditions & Withdrawal

*Draft for review. Not yet reviewed by a lawyer. CHS may change these terms at any time, with or without prior notice. Version 1.*

**Fee.** CHS charges no fee for the badge at this time. CHS may introduce a fee, or charge for renewal, by changing these terms, and will say so before charging you.

## 1. What the Badge Is and Is Not

The CHS-approved agent badge shows that CHS has checked, and approved, the company or firm named on it. CHS puts its own name behind that approval, which is why the bar is high and why we apply it strictly. The badge is not a guarantee that any transaction will succeed, not a statement that the holder works for CHS or speaks for CHS, and not a promise about the holder's future conduct. The holder stays an independent business, responsible for its own work, advice, listings, valuations and dealings. CHS gives no warranty about any of these.

## 2. Who May Apply: Registered Corporate Bodies Only

Only a corporate body may hold the badge. That means (a) a company registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) and holding an RC number; (b) a firm registered with the Estate Surveyors and Valuers Registration Board of Nigeria (ESVARBON) as a firm of estate surveyors and valuers; or (c) a firm registered with the Quantity Surveyors Registration Board of Nigeria (QSRBN) as a firm of quantity surveyors. Membership of a professional institution, such as the Nigerian Institution of Estate Surveyors and Valuers, supports an application but does not replace registration with the regulator. An individual trading in their own name, and a business name that has none of the registrations above, cannot hold the badge, however long it has traded on CHS. The application must come from a director or principal of the body, or from a person it has authorised in writing, through an agent account on CHS.

## 3. Requirement One: Complete and Current KYC

Before approval, CHS must have fully verified the person behind the agent account and every director or principal named in the application. As a minimum this means: identity verified by CHS; a valid government ID; the National Identification Number; a passed liveness check; a residential address; a working phone number and email address. The verification must be no older than 12 months. For the body itself, CHS needs its registration certificate, its registered name and number, its registered office address, and its current practising licence or renewal where the regulator issues one. CHS may ask for more, such as a utility bill, bank details, tax clearance or a site visit. Every detail must be known and verified. A missing or doubtful detail is a reason to refuse.

## 4. Requirement Two: A Real Track Record on CHS

In the 180 days before the application, and again before each renewal, the body must have taken part in at least 10 income-generating transactions on CHS, either directly with CHS or as an affiliate. A transaction counts only if CHS actually earned a fee on it that has been paid: a sale, a rental, a shortlet or hire booking or a rent-to-own deal, where the body managed the listing, paid CHS as the agent, or was paid as the listing or referring agent. Each deal counts once, however many payments it involves, such as instalments or renewals. Cancelled, refunded or unpaid deals do not count. Neither do test deals, deals with the body's own directors, staff or relatives, or deals arranged only to reach the number. CHS decides this in good faith from its own records. CHS may change the number of transactions or the number of days at any time.

## 5. Further Checks Before Approval

Meeting the numbers is not enough. Before approving, a CHS officer must confirm each of the following, and CHS keeps a record of it: (a) the ID, the NIN and the person match; (b) the residential address is real; (c) every director or principal has passed KYC; (d) the registration has been checked at source, on the CAC, ESVARBON or QSRBN register; (e) the office exists; (f) at least two independent references have been contacted; (g) no record of fraud, court judgments, regulator sanctions or upheld complaints against the body or its principals; and (h) no history of taking fees outside CHS or in breach of CHS's published fees. You consent to these checks, including contact with regulators, references, banks and other verification providers. CHS may refuse an application and need only give a short reason.

## 6. What the Holder Promises

While it holds the badge, the body promises to: give true information about every property, price and fee; charge only the fees published on CHS and take no payment outside CHS or into a personal account; show the badge only on listings it genuinely manages through CHS; keep its registration, practising licence and KYC current; tell CHS within 7 days of any change of director or principal, any change in its registration status, any sanction or disciplinary step, any court case about its business, and any insolvency step; cooperate with CHS in any complaint or dispute; follow the law and the code of conduct of its regulator, including anti-money-laundering rules and data-protection law; and treat every user fairly without discrimination.

## 7. Validity and Renewal

The badge is valid for 12 months from approval, or until the body's registration or practising licence expires, whichever comes first. The body must apply again at least 30 days before expiry and must meet every requirement afresh, including the transaction count for the 180 days before the new application. CHS sends reminders as a courtesy, but renewal is the holder's responsibility. An expired badge disappears from listings automatically.

## 8. Automatic Hiding, Suspension and Removal

The badge is hidden automatically, without any action by CHS, if it expires; if the registration or practising licence expires; if KYC becomes older than 12 months; or if the agent account is suspended or stops being verified. CHS may also suspend or remove the badge at any time, with or without prior notice, including where there is a breach of these terms, a complaint under investigation or upheld, a false or misleading statement, action by a regulator or the police, or any risk to users, to CHS or to the CHS name. Removal does not cancel money already owed, and deals already in progress continue under their own terms.

## 9. Using the Badge

The badge may be shown only as CHS shows it, on listings the body manages through CHS and in its CHS profile. The holder must not copy, change or recreate the badge, and must not use the CHS name or logo on its own adverts, signboards or documents without CHS's written approval. Anyone can check a CHS agent ID on the CHS website; a body that cannot be confirmed there is not approved. By holding the badge, the body agrees that CHS may publicly show its registered name, registration body and number, CHS agent ID, approval date and expiry date.

## 10. Information and Privacy

CHS uses the information you give for verification, for showing the badge, and for keeping users safe, under the CHS Privacy Policy. CHS may share it with regulators, law enforcement and verification partners where the law allows or requires. By naming a director or principal, you confirm that you have their consent to give their details to CHS.

## 11. Liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, CHS is not liable for any loss a person suffers from dealing with a body that holds the badge. The badge is not insurance and not a guarantee. The holder will compensate CHS for any loss CHS suffers because the holder broke these terms or gave false information. This section is a draft and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

The version in force when you apply or renew applies to that application. If you keep the badge after a change, you accept the change.
