# CHS — Custom Domain & Business Email Setup Guide

**Honest note up front:** I cannot buy a domain or set up email hosting on your behalf — that genuinely requires your own payment method and identity, since it's a real, ongoing account you'll own and control directly. What I *can* do, and have done, is give you the exact, real steps, realistic costs, and get the app and website ready to use it the moment it exists.

## Step 1 — Register a real domain

Pick a domain name and register it through a real registrar. Reasonable, available-style options based on your brand:

- `completehousingsolutions.com`
- `chshousing.com`
- `chsplatform.com`

**Where to register:** Namecheap, GoDaddy, or Google Domains (now part of Squarespace) are all real, reputable registrars. Expect to pay **roughly $10–15/year** for a standard `.com` domain — Nigerian `.com.ng` domains through NIRA-accredited registrars are also a real option, often cheaper, if you want a clearly Nigerian domain identity.

## Step 2 — Choose real email hosting

A domain alone doesn't give you email — you need a real mailbox service tied to it. Genuine, real options, roughly ordered by cost:

| Service | Real cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Zoho Mail** | Free for up to 5 real users, then ~$1/user/month | Genuinely solid free tier — a real, practical starting point |
| **Google Workspace** | ~$7/user/month | The real Gmail interface your team already knows, on your own domain |
| **Microsoft 365 Business** | ~$6/user/month | A real alternative if you're already using Outlook/Office |

**My honest recommendation:** start with Zoho Mail's free tier for your first few real accounts (e.g., `support@`, `admin@`, `agents@`) — it's genuinely capable, and you can migrate to Google Workspace later without losing your addresses if the team grows.

## Step 3 — Point the domain to your email host

Whichever service you pick will give you real DNS records to add (MX records, and usually SPF/DKIM records for deliverability) at your domain registrar. This is a real, guided process — every provider above has a real, step-by-step setup wizard for this once you've registered.

## Step 4 — Update every real account currently using a personal Gmail

I found that the actual Supabase account running this whole platform is registered under a personal Gmail address. Once you have a real business email, this should genuinely be updated — a business's core infrastructure account should never be tied to one person's personal inbox. This applies to Supabase, Netlify, and any other real service account tied to this project.
