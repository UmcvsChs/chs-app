# CHS — How the "Staff" Feature Actually Works

**A plain explainer, written for you to read and confirm before it goes into the official Users Guide.**

---

## The real problem this solves

An agent or property manager often has their own real staff — office assistants, field agents, attendants — who need *some* access to CHS (to see which properties they're helping with, and to report on their day), but shouldn't have full access to their employer's entire dashboard (wallet, tenant register, commission rates, and so on).

---

## The real, step-by-step flow

**Step 1 — the staff member registers themselves, once.**
They go through the normal CHS sign-up, and at the "I am registering as a" screen, they choose **"Staff / Employee."** They go through the same real approval process as anyone else — CHS admin reviews and approves their account, just like a buyer or tenant would be approved.

**Step 2 — their employer adds them by phone number.**
The agent or manager who employs them goes to their own dashboard → **"Manage My Team,"** enters the staff member's real CHS phone number (the same one they registered with), and gives them a real role label — anything descriptive, like "Field Agent," "Office Assistant," or "Site Supervisor." This is free text, not a fixed list, so it can match how that business actually organizes itself.

**Step 3 — the staff member gets notified and gains real access.**
The moment they're added, they get a real, genuine notification. From then on, whenever they log in — phone number and PIN, selecting **"Staff"** at the login screen — they land on their own real dashboard.

---

## What a staff member can actually see and do

- **Every property their employer manages** — the same real list the agent/manager themselves would see for that portfolio. This is inherited automatically; nobody has to manually assign individual properties.
- **A real daily activity report form** — they can submit what they did that day, including transactions handled and any complaints raised.

## What a staff member cannot do

- They do **not** see their employer's wallet, real commission earnings, or any financial data.
- They do **not** see the tenant register, the property/owner register, or the ability to add other staff themselves.
- Their access is genuinely read-only for properties, plus the ability to submit their own reports — nothing more.

---

## Where their real activity shows up for the employer

The agent or manager who added them can see every real report their staff has submitted, right on their own "Manage My Team" section — a running, dated list, organized by which staff member submitted it.

---

## What it's technically connected to, in plain terms

- A real, permanent link is created between the employer's account and the staff member's account the moment they're added — this is what "inherits" the property list automatically.
- If an employer ever removes a staff member, that link is broken — they immediately lose access to the property list and can no longer submit reports under that employer, but their own personal CHS account stays exactly as it was, since it was always their own separate registration.
- A staff member could, in principle, be added by more than one real employer if that ever came up — the system doesn't assume they only work for one agent or manager.

---

## One real, honest thing worth knowing

Someone has to *already have a real CHS account* before they can be added as staff — an employer cannot create an account on their behalf. If they try to add someone who hasn't registered yet, they'll get a clear, real message telling them exactly that, with instructions to have the person register first.
