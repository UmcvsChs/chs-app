How it works

From browsing to keys, in a real, visible sequence.

1

Browse real, checked listings

Search by state, area, price, and property type across sale, rent, lease, hire, shortlet, and rent-to-own — every listing reviewed before it's shown.

2

Make a real offer or application

A buyer proposes a price directly to the owner; a tenant submits a rental application that CHS screens before it ever reaches the landlord.

3

See the real total, before you pay anything

A transparent breakdown — the price, the real commission, the combined total — shown in advance, charged once, never as a surprise second charge.

4

Funds held until the real handover completes

For a sale, your payment sits safely in escrow until CHS confirms the real legal documents have genuinely reached you.

5

A real record, for as long as you need it

Receipts, condition reports, and message history stay on file — real evidence if a question ever comes up later.
