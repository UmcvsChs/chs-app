If this happens, CHS's commission remains legally owed by both the Owner/Seller and the Buyer/Tenant, and CHS reserves the right to sue for recovery of that commission plus costs.

3. Commission structure. CHS's commission differs by transaction type. On a rental or lease, CHS charges 5% from the Tenant and 5.5% from the Landlord/Owner. On a sale, CHS charges 6.5% from the Buyer and 6% from the Owner/Seller. No inspection fee is charged as standard — where genuine transport costs apply, they are disclosed upfront and split between both sides. Caution fees apply only where the Owner has specifically requested one.

4. Honesty and accurate information is mandatory. Owners must genuinely hold or represent the rights to list a property. Falsified documents, misrepresented ownership, or fraudulent listings result in permanent suspension and may be reported to law enforcement.

5. Agents and Property Managers must not extort Users. No agent may demand undisclosed fees, caution money, or inspection charges outside what CHS has published. Violation results in permanent suspension.

6. All funds are held in escrow until the conditions for release are met — meaning no party is paid before the other party confirms the transaction is complete.

7. Disputes are resolved through CHS's internal process first, before resorting to arbitration or the courts of Kaduna State.

8. CHS reserves the right to suspend or terminate any account found in breach of these terms, and to pursue civil or criminal remedies for fraud, circumvention, or misconduct.

10. How the Agent Referral system works

Every registered agent has a unique code (e.g. CHS-AG-0024), automatically attached to every property link generated from their dashboard.

Where the link goes. A referral link always opens the property directly on CHS — never on the agent's personal page, profile, or any third-party site. This is intentional: it means CHS can verify the transaction and calculate commission accurately, and it means the buyer/tenant always transacts through CHS's protections (escrow, dispute resolution, document verification), regardless of where they first saw the link.

Where an agent can share it. Anywhere the agent already has an audience — their own Facebook page, WhatsApp status, Instagram bio, a physical flyer with a short link, and so on. Sharing the link doesn't move any part of the transaction off CHS; it only brings the visitor to CHS.

How commission is credited. If someone clicks the link, browses, and eventually completes a transaction on that property (rent, sale, or lease), CHS automatically attributes the deal to that agent's code and credits their commission — no manual claim or extra step required from the agent.

What this is not. This is not a way to direct buyers/tenants to complete a deal outside CHS — see term 2 above. Attempting to circumvent CHS after using a referral link is treated the same as any other circumvention attempt.
