# 527 One notification per checkout (applied live 527a helpers, then a patch of buy_product_direct and checkout_cart)
During a cart checkout, buy_product_direct skips its own per-line notifications (flag chs.batch_checkout, local to the transaction). checkout_cart sends: one summary to the admins (new function _notify_checkout_summary, title "New marketplace order: N items from M sellers", body lists each seller's shipment and items) and one "Prepare this order" notification per seller listing that seller's items (_checkout_items_text). The per-shipment admin notification was removed. Single "buy now" purchases are unchanged. Tested: 20 items from 8 sellers gave 9 notifications instead of 56.
